# Customer Board Support

We can support your hardware in two ways:

1. **Check for existing support** – See if your board is already listed on our [supported devices list](https://github.com/balena-io/docs/blob/main/pages/reference/hardware/devices/README.md).
2. **Request custom support** – If you need an alternative to our existing options, explore our [Custom Device Support (CDS) service](https://www.balena.io/pricing). This paid service includes a custom balenaOS build for your hardware and ongoing maintenance for a monthly fee.
3. **Custom support requirements**- for more information on the technical requirements for custom support please [see this page](../../learn/more/custom-device-support.md).

{% hint style="info" %}
We do not currently support customers who wish to bring up balenaOS on new hardware themselves.
{% endhint %}
